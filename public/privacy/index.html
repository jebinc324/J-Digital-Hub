Privacy Policy for Just24Things

Read how the Just24Things static website and third-party services may handle information.

Overview

Just24Things is a publicly accessible website featuring content, browser games and links to software downloads. The site currently does not require an account to browse its public pages.

Information and hosting

When you visit the site, the hosting provider and your browser may process technical information such as IP addresses, request logs and device/browser information to deliver the website and maintain security. We do not claim that hosting logs are disabled.

Local browser storage

Site features such as theme preferences or browser games may store settings or progress locally in your browser. You can clear this data using your browser settings.

External websites

Links to YouTube, GitHub, Google Fonts and other third-party services are governed by their respective privacy policies. Following an external link may allow that service to collect information independently.

Downloads and Android apps

Individual Android apps may have different data practices and permissions. Review the app's own documentation and Android permission prompts before installing or using it. This page describes the public website, not every app.

Changes and questions

This policy may be updated as the website changes. For publicly listed contact methods, see our Contact page.
